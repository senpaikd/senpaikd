## Featured projects

### [Documind](https://github.com/senpaikd/documind)

Ask questions about PDFs, text files, and Markdown, then inspect the passages and page references behind the answers.<br>
`Next.js` `TypeScript` · [Live demo](https://documind-bay-kappa.vercel.app/)

### [HelpDesk](https://github.com/senpaikd/Helpdesk-ticketing)

Customers submit and track support requests in a private portal. Staff manage assignments, replies, and ticket history from a shared queue.<br>
`Laravel` `PHP` `MySQL`

Employees clock in and out for scheduled workdays, while administrators manage accounts and attendance records.<br>
`Laravel` `PHP` `MySQL`
